# About me

I'm Danial Zendehdel, a research engineer at [Honda Research Institute Europe](https://www.honda-ri.de/) in Offenbach and a PhD candidate in electronics engineering at Sapienza University of Rome, defending in October 2026.

My work is deep reinforcement learning for sequential decision making under uncertainty: continuous and discrete action spaces, multi-objective and constrained formulations, and multi-agent settings. In the PhD I applied it to battery dispatch and real-time energy management for renewable energy communities, with a particular interest in policies that account for battery degradation rather than optimising cost alone. At Honda I work on multi-agent coordination under shared resource constraints, currently fair electric-vehicle charging under a shared power budget. In between, as a guest researcher at TU Delft, I built a reinforcement learning environment for the long-term energy transition of the Netherlands at national scale.

Before energy systems I worked in computer vision and robotics at EPFL's VITA lab: deep stereo networks for depth estimation on unrectified 360-degree images, a new lidar-and-camera dataset, and a real-time person detection and tracking pipeline running on a Loomo mobile robot.

I write mostly in Python and PyTorch, with C/C++, MATLAB/Simulink and ROS where the hardware needs it.

See the [CV](/cv/) for details, the [Projects](/projects/) page for write-ups, and the [Posts](/posts/) for notes on reinforcement learning and the occasional infrastructure walkthrough.
