## Overview

Energy management in a smart grid rests on a demand forecast. Household demand has strong daily and weekly seasonality, and it is not obvious whether models that model seasonality explicitly beat general-purpose ones once tuned. This study compares the two families on real demand time series.

## Approach

- Seasonal and non-seasonal forecasting techniques trained and evaluated on the same energy-demand series and horizons.
- Common preprocessing and error metrics so that the comparison isolates the modelling choice.

## Results

The comparison quantifies when explicit seasonality helps and when it does not. Published at IJCCI 2023.
