## Overview

A Segway Loomo robot should pick one person out of a crowd and follow them. Everything, detection, tracking and control, has to run on the robot's own computer with no offboard help.

## Approach

- A person detection and tracking pipeline deployed on the Loomo's onboard computer.
- Real-time inference budget respected so the control loop keeps up with a walking person.

## Results

The robot followed a chosen person in real time with all inference onboard.
