## Overview

Long-term energy planning asks a sequence of questions: what to build, what to retrofit, and when, while demand, prices and technology costs remain uncertain for decades. This project frames that as a sequential decision problem at national scale and trains reinforcement learning agents to solve it.

## Approach

- A reinforcement learning environment that steps through the long-term energy transition of the Netherlands year by year, covering renewable integration and system planning.
- Multi-year investment and retrofitting choices modelled as actions; uncertainty in demand and costs modelled in the transition dynamics.
- Agents trained inside the environment to learn multi-year investment and retrofitting policies.

## Results

The environment and trained agents provide a testbed for comparing planning policies for the Dutch energy transition under uncertainty.
